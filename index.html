Who is Jesus?

A story of love, grace and hope.

Christians believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God and the Saviour of the world. His life, teachings, death and resurrection are at the centre of the Christian faith.

Jesus welcomed people, cared for those who were overlooked, taught about God's Kingdom and called people to love God and their neighbours.

What did Jesus teach?

Teachings that shape Christian life

Jesus taught people to love God, care for others, forgive, serve and live with humility.

♡

Love your neighbour

Treat others with kindness, dignity and compassion, including people who are different from you.

✧

Forgiveness

Jesus taught people to forgive and to seek reconciliation rather than hold on to bitterness.

☼

Humility

Greatness is not about showing off. Jesus taught that serving others matters.

♡

Compassion

Notice people who are struggling and respond with practical care and generosity.

✝

Faith

Trust God, seek truth and build a relationship with Him through prayer and Scripture.

☁

Hope

The Christian message says that God's love and grace offer hope even in difficult times.

The story of Jesus

A journey through His life

Here is a simple overview of the major events described in the four Gospels.

01 · BIRTH

Born in Bethlehem

The Gospels of Matthew and Luke tell of Jesus' birth and the events surrounding it.

02 · EARLY LIFE

Growing up

Luke describes Jesus growing up in Nazareth and visiting Jerusalem as a boy.

03 · MINISTRY

Teaching and miracles

Jesus travelled, taught about God's Kingdom, healed people and called disciples.

04 · THE CROSS

Death in Jerusalem

The Gospels describe Jesus' arrest, trial and crucifixion.

05 · RESURRECTION

The empty tomb

The Gospels proclaim that Jesus rose from the dead, a central belief of Christianity.

06 · THE MISSION

A message for the world

Jesus' followers were commissioned to share His teachings and make disciples.

New to the Bible?

How to start reading

You don't have to read everything at once. Start small and take time to think about what you discover.

1

Choose a Gospel

John is a popular starting point if you want to explore who Jesus is.

2

Read a little

Try one chapter or a short passage. Take your time with it.

3

Ask questions

Think about what the passage says, what it means and what you want to explore further.

Prayer and reflection

You can talk to God

Prayer does not need complicated words. Christians believe you can speak honestly to God.

A simple prayer

“Dear God, thank You for Your love. Help me to understand Jesus and His teachings. Guide me to live with kindness, courage, forgiveness and faith. Help me grow in wisdom and love. Amen.”

You can use your own words too.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What are the four Gospels?

Matthew, Mark, Luke and John are the first four books of the New Testament. They each tell the story of Jesus and His teachings.

Do I need to pay to read the Bible?

No. There are free online Bible resources. The links on this page open the King James Version on Bible Gateway.

Which Gospel should I read first?

You could start with Mark for a shorter, direct account, or John for a focused exploration of Jesus' identity and message.

What is Christianity about?

Christianity is centred on belief in God and Jesus Christ. Christians follow Jesus' teachings and believe His death and resurrection bring forgiveness and hope.

Do I have to understand everything immediately?

No. It is fine to take your time, ask questions and discuss what you read with a trusted person or faith leader.
